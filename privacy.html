Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Overview

Lumvi ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies, please do not use our service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a Lumvi account, we collect your name, email address, date of birth, phone number, and password. We also collect profile information you voluntarily provide, including photos, bio, location, occupation, and interests.

Verification Information

To verify your identity, we collect government-issued ID information and facial images. This data is used only for verification purposes and is not shared with other users.

Assessment Data

Your responses to personality assessments, compatibility questions, and values assessments are collected to power our matching algorithm and provide personalized guidance through Lumvi.

Communication Data

We collect messages you send through Lumvi, including conversations with our AI coach and with other users. This data is used to improve our service, provide support, and ensure compliance with our safety policies.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with Lumvi, including IP address, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent, and interaction patterns.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our matchmaking service
  • To verify your identity and ensure account security
  • To power our AI matching algorithm and compatibility scoring
  • To provide personalized coaching through Lumvi
  • To communicate with you about your account and our service
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To develop new features and services

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Shared With Other Users

Profile information, photos, and messages are shared with other users only when both parties mutually express interest in connecting. Personal contact information (phone, email, home address) is never shared unless you explicitly consent.

Service Providers

We share data with third-party service providers who assist us (payment processors, cloud storage providers, verification services). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and authorized to use your information only as necessary to provide our services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and security measures to protect your data. However, no security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Active Accounts: We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Deleted Accounts: Upon account deletion, we retain a de-identified backup for 90 days, then permanently delete your data. Some information may be retained if required by law.

Communication Logs: Conversations between users are retained for 2 years to handle disputes and ensure safety compliance.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Restrict processing of your data
  • Port your data to another service
  • Opt-out of specific data uses

To exercise these rights, contact us via our contact form.

8. Children's Privacy

Lumvi is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with information, we will immediately delete it and terminate the account.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

10. Third-Party Links

Lumvi may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before providing information.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes become effective when posted. Your continued use of Lumvi constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or to exercise your rights, contact us via our contact form.
