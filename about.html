About Lumvi

Lumvi is a values-driven life partner matchmaking platform for people who are done with endless swiping and looking for something real.

We believe that lasting relationships are built on shared values, genuine compatibility, and intentional connection—not algorithms optimized for engagement or swipe volume.

Our Mission

To help people find long-lasting, meaningful partnerships with someone they can build a full, abundant life with. We do this by:

Matching on what actually matters

We measure over 100 compatibility signals across personal values, personality, attachment style, life goals, faith, finances, and more. You see exactly why you match.

Introducing before revealing

Our 7-day blind introduction lets you fall for who someone is before how they look. This prevents physical attraction from overriding deeper compatibility.

Guiding every step of the way

Lumvi, our AI coach, is with you from self-discovery through meeting your match. They provide emotional support, relationship guidance, and help navigate every stage with wisdom.

Curating instead of enabling endless choice

You receive 1-3 carefully vetted connections per month. This creates intentionality. Fewer options mean higher quality matches.

Why Lumvi Exists

The dating app market is flooded with platforms optimized for engagement—more swipes, more notifications, endless browsing. This creates a paradox of choice that makes people less satisfied with their connections.

Meanwhile, serious people looking for life partners feel overwhelmed and exhausted. They're swiping through hundreds of profiles, most of whom are just browsing, and struggling to find anyone with compatible values.

Lumvi exists for the people done with that. For people who want to invest in meaningful connections with genuinely compatible partners. For people who value depth over volume.

Our Values

Honesty over hype

We tell you exactly how our matching algorithm works, how your data is used, and what you can expect. No mystery. No dark patterns designed to addict you.

Safety as non-negotiable

Every member is ID and photo verified. We have zero tolerance for fraud, harassment, or abuse. Your safety is our responsibility.

Quality over quantity

We'd rather facilitate one meaningful connection than 100 superficial matches. We measure success by relationships, not by engagement metrics.

Privacy by design

Your data is yours. We never sell it to third parties or use it to track you outside our platform. Everything is encrypted, and you control what you share.

The Team

Lumvi was built by people who believe in the power of values-driven connection. We combine expertise in matchmaking algorithms, AI coaching, relationship psychology, and technology to create a platform that actually works.

We're backed by investors and advisors who share our commitment to helping people find genuine, lasting partnerships.

Looking Forward
