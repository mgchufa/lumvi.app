Safety & Security

Last Updated: May 2026

At Lumvi, safety is our highest priority. We've designed our platform with comprehensive safeguards to protect our community from fraud, abuse, and harmful behavior.

Verification System

🆔 ID Verification

Every member is ID verified before their profile becomes active. We partner with industry-leading identity verification providers to confirm that users are who they claim to be. This includes checking government-issued IDs against liveness checks to prevent catfishing and impersonation.

📸 Photo Verification

All profile photos are verified to match the user's actual appearance through our facial recognition system. Photos that don't match the ID or show suspicious editing are rejected. This prevents deception and ensures accurate representation.

✅ Profile Completeness

Incomplete or minimal profiles are not shown to potential matches. We require thoughtful, complete profiles because research shows that serious members with complete profiles are more likely to form genuine connections. Low-effort profiles are more likely to be fraudulent.

Community Protection

🛡️ Fraud Detection

We use advanced machine learning to detect suspicious patterns including romance scams, catfishing, payment fraud, and bot activity. Our systems monitor accounts continuously and flag suspicious behavior for human review.

🚨 Report & Block Features

Users can report inappropriate behavior, spam, or safety concerns with one click. Our safety team reviews all reports within 24 hours. Users can block other members at any time to prevent further contact.

⛔ Zero-Tolerance Policy

We have a strict zero-tolerance policy for sexual harassment, threats, abuse, or any behavior that violates our community standards. Violations result in immediate account termination. Serious offenses are reported to law enforcement.

💬 Moderated Interactions

Our AI system scans conversations for potential safety concerns, including grooming language, explicit solicitation, and suspicious financial requests. This helps us intervene before harm occurs.

Data Protection

🔐 Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest. Personal information like phone numbers and addresses are stored securely and never displayed publicly unless you explicitly choose to share them.

🔑 Password Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard bcrypt hashing. We recommend using unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication for your account.

🔍 Limited Data Sharing

Personal contact information is never shared between users unless both parties mutually opt-in. Even after mutual interest, you control what information you reveal and when.

Smart Design Choices

7-Day Blind Introduction

Research shows that physical attraction can override judgment when people see photos first. By introducing users to who someone is before how they look, it reduces superficial rejections and helps people form genuine impressions based on values and personality—not catfishing.

Curated Not Endless

3 carefully matched connections per month means members take each connection seriously. This reduces the "swipe culture" mentality and creates more intentional interactions. Fewer matches = higher quality matches = less likelihood of encountering bad actors.

Guided by Lumvi

Lumvi, our AI coach, is present at every stage of the journey. Lumvi can recognize warning signs in conversations and nudge users toward healthy communication. If something feels off, you can always ask Lumvi for guidance.

User Responsibilities

Safety is a shared responsibility. We ask all members to:

  • Be honest: Provide accurate information about yourself in your profile
  • Be respectful: Treat other members with dignity and kindness
  • Communicate clearly: If you're not interested, say so gracefully. Ghosting is not tolerated here.
  • Trust your instincts: If something feels off about a member, trust that feeling and report it
  • Never share sensitive info: Don't share financial information, passwords, or personal details early on
  • Meet in public: Always meet first dates in public places during daylight hours
  • Tell someone: Let a trusted friend know where you're going and when you'll be back
  • Report bad behavior: Help us maintain a safe community by reporting members who violate our rules

Safety Resources

In-App Support

Lumvi can provide guidance on healthy communication, red flags, and relationship advice. You can ask Lumvi questions about safety at any time during your journey.

Blocking & Reporting

You can block or report any member instantly. All reports are reviewed by our safety team, and action is taken within 24 hours.

Post-Meeting Check-ins

After meeting someone in person, Lumvi checks in with both members to ensure everything went well. This helps us identify and address any issues early.

Law Enforcement Cooperation

Lumvi cooperates fully with law enforcement and regulatory bodies. We provide requested information in response to valid legal orders. If we become aware of criminal activity, we report it to appropriate authorities.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have safety concerns or need support, contact us immediately via our contact form. Our safety team is available to help.

Your safety is non-negotiable. We're committed to maintaining the safest values-driven dating community.
