How It Works

Four Stages. One Outcome.

Every connection follows a guided path designed to build real intimacy, not just chemistry.

1
Days 1–7
The Blind Introduction

Lumvi introduces your match through daily notes, what they love, how they'd treat you, how they handle conflict, before you ever see their photo. You fall for the person, not the profile.

2
Mutual Interest
Profile Reveal

If both sides express interest after the introduction, profiles are revealed simultaneously. You see the full picture, photo, career, location, and your complete personalized compatibility breakdown.

3
Mutual Opt-In
Guided Conversations

Lumvi guided conversations with your connection. Real getting-to-know-you conversations without the pressure of an immediate first date.

4
Mutual Opt-In
Meeting in Person

When both people are ready, Lumvi helps you plan your first date. If either of you isn't ready, Lumvi guides your communication with honesty and kindness. Nobody gets ghosted here.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Lumvi isn't a dating app. It is a platform that curates meaningful long lasting connections. You don't swipe. You don't browse. You receive 1-3 deeply vetted introductions per month, guided by an AI coach who knows your full profile. The process is slower and more intentional by design. We optimize for long-term compatibility, not engagement time.
Lumvi is your confidant and guide during your journey. A persistent conversational companion equipped with understanding of the latest knowledge in relationship success and longevity. They remember every dialogue, learn how you're emotionally wired over time, and guide you through every stage of your journey: self-discovery, connection introductions, guided conversations, and post-meeting check-ins. They are at the core of the service we offer.
Research consistently shows that physical attraction can override deeper compatibility signals when people see a photo first. By introducing you to who someone is before how they look, you form a genuine impression grounded in values, personality, and connection. Profiles are only revealed when both sides express mutual interest after 7 days of thoughtful introduction.
You can pass at any point in the process. If you decline after showing interest, Lumvi helps you write a kind and constructive message to your connection. Nobody gets ghosted on Lumvi. Graceful exits are a core part of how we work.
Every member completed ID and photo verification before their profile is made available for connections. We also require profile completeness — members with incomplete profiles simply aren't shown as matches until they finish. This keeps memberships high-quality for everyone.
Yes. Your profile, assessment data, and conversations are encrypted and never sold to third parties. Personal contact information is never shared between members until both parties mutually opt into meeting in person or consent to share their information to third party providers for gifts and or event bookings. We take your safety seriously at every layer.
