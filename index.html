How It Works

Four stages. One direction.

Every connection follows a guided path designed to build real intimacy — not just chemistry.

1
Days 1–14
The Blind Introduction

Sage introduces your match through daily notes — what they love, how they'd treat you, how they handle conflict — before you ever see their photo. You fall for the person, not the profile.

2
Mutual Interest
The Profile Reveal

If both sides express interest after the introduction, profiles are revealed simultaneously. You see the full picture — photo, occupation, location, and your complete compatibility breakdown.

3
Minimum 2 Weeks
AI-Coached Conversation

Guided messaging with Sage-suggested prompts. No personal info shared yet. Real getting-to-know-you conversations without the pressure of a first date. Minimum two weeks before the next stage unlocks.

4
Mutual Opt-In
Meeting in Person

When both people are ready, Sage helps you plan your first meeting. If either of you isn't ready, Sage helps you communicate that with honesty and kindness. Nobody gets ghosted here.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Lumvi isn't a dating app — it's a matchmaking platform. You don't swipe. You don't browse. You receive 3–5 deeply vetted introductions per month, guided by an AI coach who knows your full profile. The process is slower and more intentional by design. We optimize for long-term compatibility, not engagement time.
Sage is your AI matchmaking coach — a persistent conversational companion powered by Claude (Anthropic). She remembers every conversation, learns how you're emotionally wired over time, and coaches you through every stage of your journey: self-discovery, match introductions, guided conversations, and meeting in person. She's the core of the product, not an add-on.
Research consistently shows that physical attraction can override deeper compatibility signals when people see a photo first. By introducing you to who someone is before how they look, you form a genuine impression grounded in values, personality, and connection. Profiles are only revealed when both sides express mutual interest — after 14 days of thoughtful introduction.
You can pass at any point in the process. If you decline after showing interest, Sage helps you write a kind and constructive message to your match. Nobody gets ghosted on Lumvi. Graceful exits are a core part of how we work.
Every member completes ID and photo verification before their profile is made available for matching. We also score profile completeness — members with incomplete profiles simply aren't shown as matches until they finish. This keeps the pool high-quality for everyone.
Yes. Your profile, assessment data, and coaching conversations are encrypted and never sold to third parties. Personal contact information is never shared between members until both parties mutually opt into meeting in person. We take your safety seriously at every layer.
